## What Zhot sounds like

- Melodies that linger: immediate hooks with emotional payoff.
- Textures with personality: organic instruments + subtle electronic warmth.
- Lyrics rooted in hope: storytelling that nudges listeners from negativity to positive action.

## Why Zhot exists (mission)

Small daily rituals beat big noisy campaigns. Zhot’s strategy: seed the world with repeatable micro-actions (tiny-share formats, playlist-first outreach, multilingual storytelling) that build long-term discovery without pay-to-play.

## How collaborators can work with Zhot

- Curators: we provide single-track smartlinks, stems for remixes, and short curator notes explaining context & fit.
- Journalists: pitch packages include short bios, streaming data snapshots, and three story hooks (artist journey, community angle, campaign tie-in).
